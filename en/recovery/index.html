Do this first

  1. Don't save anything to that card. New files can overwrite what was deleted.
  2. Don't format it or run an error check. Press Cancel if Windows asks.
  3. Take the card out of your dashcam or camera. Otherwise it keeps recording over the space.

What it can't do

  • Space already overwritten by other files can't be recovered. Check the free scan first.
  • Only SD cards and USB sticks that appear with a drive letter (D: etc.) are supported.
  • Internal or external hard drives, SSDs, phone internal storage and devices your PC doesn't detect are not supported.

Questions

When do I pay?

Scanning and previews are free, and you can save up to 10 found files for free. To save more, buy a recovery pass for that one card or USB stick.

Can it find photos on a formatted SD card?

Deep scan looks for photo and video pieces left in free space. Files get new names, and space already overwritten cannot be recovered.

Does it work with phone storage or hard drives?

No. It supports SD cards and USB sticks that appear with a drive letter such as D:.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Everything happens on your PC and no account is needed.
